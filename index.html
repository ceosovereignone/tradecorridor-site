Trade support and transaction processing software for physical commodity trading desks.

Corridor is being built to help trading desks run the operational side of physical trades — from confirmation through settlement — in one structured workflow.

The problem

Physical commodity trading desks coordinate a chain of operational steps for every trade: confirmations, nominations, balancing, invoicing, and reconciliation.

Today that work is typically spread across spreadsheets, email, and disconnected tools. Manual handoffs between steps are slow and introduce operational risk.

The product

Corridor has two functions:

  • Trade support — market analysis, legal and contracting support, shipping and logistics.
  • Transaction processing and settlement automation — structured workflows for confirmations, nomination scheduling, balancing, invoicing, and reconciliation.

Initial focus: piped gas.

Corridor is software. It is not a clearing house.
